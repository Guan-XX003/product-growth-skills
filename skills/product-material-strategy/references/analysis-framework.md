# Product + Material Analysis Framework

Use this reference when creating or substantially rewriting a product analysis / material strategy document.

## First Principle

Write as a director / paid-media creative analyst. The document should answer:

- What user problem can the product become a video hook for?
- Which product facts can be visually proven in the first few seconds?
- Which audience pools and content pools should be tested?
- Which existing materials can be copied for volume, and which new shells should be tested for expansion?

Do not write like a traditional product manager. Do not make product-improvement suggestions the center of the document unless the user asks.

## 第一节：产品分析

Use this section order unless the user gives a different structure.

### 1. 产品基础信息

Explain what the product is in one paragraph from a marketing-material perspective:

- Product category, platform, official positioning, and key public facts.
- What users can understand in the first 1-3 seconds.
- What real demand the product enters: self-motivation, scene demand, social demand, learning demand, efficiency demand, emotional demand, etc.
- Which visible result is easier to sell than the full feature list.

Pattern:

`[Product] is not only [surface category]. In paid video materials, the best entry point is [specific user problem/result], not [abstract feature list].`

For knowledge/content products, explicitly distinguish:

- Self-motivation: self-improvement, growth anxiety, internal friction, discipline, cognition upgrade.
- Scene demand: workplace, social expression, education, parenting, finance, decision-making, relationship, concrete problem solving.

### 2. 产品功能

Group functions by material value, not by product architecture.

| 功能模块 | 具体功能 | 营销素材价值 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First hook function | The most visible or easiest-to-understand function | Can it stop users in the first 3 seconds? |
| Differentiating function | AI, automation, conversation, personalization, creation, etc. | Why this product is not just a generic category app |
| Library/content depth | Templates, books, assets, categories, updated lists | Supports series and long-tail audience segmentation |
| Scene function | Commute, sleep, work, parenting, fitness, travel, etc. | Lets the video look like real life instead of a UI demo |
| Retention/support function | Notes, saved highlights, daily recommendation, history, reminders | Belongs mid/late video unless it is the core hook |
| Monetization/entitlement | Subscription, trial, premium assets, usage quota | Mention carefully; do not create free/paid mismatch |

### 3. 产品卖点

Do not only list features. Split selling points by their job in the material.

#### 3.1 主卖点：最适合放在素材前 3 秒的核心吸引点

| 优先级 | 主卖点 | 卖点拆解 | 适合的素材表达 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 第一优先级 | Strongest user result | What the user is actually buying, such as time saved, status, confidence, answers, visible result | Short hook line and first-screen visual proof |
| 第一优先级 | Directional use case | What exact problem this solves | "When you have [problem], use [product] to [result]" |
| 第二优先级 | Differentiator | Why this is not the same as a common alternative | Demo or comparison after the hook |

For knowledge/content products, good primary selling point types include:

- 最短时间拿到最有价值的信息
- 指向型学习
- 一个现实问题对应一本书/一类内容
- 听完/看完后还能继续追问
- 替代低效信息摄入

Avoid making "large library" or "can listen/read" the only main selling point when competitors already own that mindshare.

#### 3.2 次卖点：适合做系列化素材的内容方向

| 卖点方向 | 用户为什么会被打动 | 素材价值 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-improvement | Wants to improve but lacks time or path | Growth, discipline, workplace, cognition material |
| Emotion/internal friction | Wants methods, not just comfort | Emotional hook must connect to an actionable method |
| Scene problem | Needs a tool in work/social/parenting/finance moments | Works well for short-drama or scenario shells |
| AI/conversation | One-way content becomes follow-up learning | Good for new-type test, not only a closing feature |

### 4. 竞品分析：功能特点、优劣势与重合用户画像

Make competitor analysis a separate section before industry pain points. The purpose is not to prove the product should become the competitor; it is to understand what user mindshare is already occupied, what functions users will compare, what this product wins/loses on, and which user groups overlap.

Use public sources for competitor positioning and cite them when writing a live document.

| 竞品/类别 | 竞品功能特点 | 本产品相比竞品的优势 | 本产品相比竞品的劣势/压力 | 重合用户画像人群 | 对素材的启发 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mature platform competitor | Public features and user mindshare | Where this product is sharper, faster, more scenario-specific, or easier to prove | Where the competitor is stronger in trust, habit, content, price, or ecosystem | Real user groups both products may reach | What the material should avoid, borrow, or counter-position |
| Direct feature competitor | Same headline benefit or direct substitute | Product-specific differentiator | Mature category education or stronger brand trust | Directly overlapping demand groups | Push the product's sharper differentiator |
| Adjacent behavior competitor | Solves the same user need differently | Shorter path or more visible result | Stronger attention habit/community/depth | Adjacent demand groups | Show why this product is a shorter path for the specific problem |

For knowledge/content products, useful competitor categories include:

- Audio platforms: strong in "listening content" and long companionship.
- Reading platforms: strong in complete reading, reading identity, notes, and book community.
- Podcast/blog platforms: strong in human voice, emotional companionship, and topic discussion.
- Knowledge-payment/course platforms: strong in systems, experts, and paid learning identity.
- Direct summary/micro-learning competitors: strong in short summaries and habit learning.

### 5. 行业痛点分析：基于竞品与同大类共性问题

Keep this section about same-category industry pain points. It should be based on the competitor/category analysis above, not a mixed list of competitor names. Do not turn this into product-improvement advice unless the user asks.

| 行业痛点 | 同大类产品的共性问题 | 对素材的影响 | 表达注意事项 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shallow result risk | "This may just be a shallow summary/demo/template." | Reframe as first-step, screening, or problem-solving aid | Avoid absolute claims like "fully replaces..." |
| Trust gap | "AI/ad/demo may be fake or inaccurate." | Show source, real screen, real prompt, real result | Avoid magic/omniscient AI framing |
| Category misconception | "Is this just another generic app?" | Open with a concrete problem and visible product path | Show the product's unique sequence fast |
| Hook-product break | "The hook is interesting but I do not see why I need the app." | Connect emotion/scenario to product within 3-5 seconds | Do not let the whole video stay as pure sentiment |
| Free/paid mismatch | "Will I download and hit a paywall?" | Clarify value before entitlement; avoid over-free promises | Confirm landing page and app experience |
| Localization/landing mismatch | "The ad is in my language but the product is not." | Use real localized UI and content when possible | Do not imply coverage that does not exist |
| Library/content fit | "Will I find what I came for?" | Use theme/scene-based content sets, not only one item | Ensure featured content exists in the product |
| Compliance/rules risk | "The account/material may be restricted." | Build a rule library for risky words/claims/assets | Especially important after account bans |

### 6. 产品用户人群分析

This section must be concrete enough to guide material selection and audience testing. Do not stop at "age + needs".

Default rule: do not create one row for each of the eight Douyin e-commerce audiences. First define real user groups that may actually have demand, then map each group to the relevant strategy audiences. If a strategy audience has no clear demand, do not force it into the table.

Douyin e-commerce eight strategy audiences:

- Z世代
- 新锐白领
- 精致妈妈
- 资深中产
- 都市蓝领
- 小镇青年
- 都市银发
- 小镇中老年

| 真实用户群体/年龄 | 对应八大人群归类 | 真实生活场景/状态 | 对产品的核心需求 | 内容池可能感兴趣的视频类型 | 电商池可能购买的其他品类 | 素材方向判断 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User group + age range, such as 出境旅行/留学人群，18-35 | Relevant categories only, such as Z世代、新锐白领、资深中产 | Work/life rhythm, pressure, fragmented time, current behavior | Trigger and the job to be done | Douyin/TikTok content they likely consume | Adjacent e-commerce categories they may buy | Hook, scene, proof, and product path |

Mark content-pool and e-commerce-pool entries as material-testing hypotheses unless they come from actual platform data.

For knowledge/content products, useful audience types include:

- Managers/founders who lack time but need business judgment.
- Senior workers stuck in career bottlenecks.
- Young workers who want discipline but need a lighter start.
- Education-anxious parents.
- Upward-mobility young people in emerging markets.
- High-knowledge/collector readers.
- Emotionally exhausted young users who want practical methods.
- Finance-anxious learners.

## 第二节：现有爆款视频素材分析

### 无现成案例时：公开视频素材类型参考

用户未提供现有爆款素材或投放数据时，主动寻找公开视频做初步参考；只有用户明确要求只留框架时才留空。国外产品优先搜索 TikTok、YouTube，使用目标市场语言和当地使用场景。先找本产品，再找竞品，最后扩展到同人群、同需求的跨品类内容；清楚标明每条与本产品的关系。

此时第二节标题改为“第二节：公开视频素材类型参考（初步）”，按以下结构记录：

1. 视频信息：真实可访问的原视频链接、平台、作者、发布日期（若可见）、采集日期、语言/市场、视频时长（若可见）。说明是短视频还是长视频，以及本产品、竞品或跨品类参考。
2. 可见表现：只记录页面实际显示的播放、点赞、评论等数据；看不到就注明未公开或未获取。不同平台、发布时间与长短视频不直接按播放量排名；公开互动不能推导广告消耗、CTR、CVR、CPA、留存或回收。
3. 素材类型与结构：开头怎样吸引人、针对谁和什么需求、如何展开、展示了什么产品结果。实际看过视频才能分析镜头、节奏、字幕和声音；只拿到标题、摘要或文字稿时明确局限，不补写未看到的画面。
4. 可借鉴与迁移：说明可复用的内容形式、适合本产品的展示方式、不能照搬的能力与场景；初步判断写成待测试方向，不称为已验证的爆款逻辑。
5. 后续验证：保留位置，供用户补充实际素材、投放截图与数据；有真实数据后再沿用下面的完整案例结构分析。

每条参考都应能追溯到具体视频，而非只有搜索页、频道或凭空举例。平台访问受限时如实说明，继续寻找另一平台上能核验的案例；仅有搜索线索时单列“待核验线索”，不当作已分析案例。参考分析不等于获得下载、转载或混剪授权。


Use one repeated case structure. Preserve the user's modified structure if they have established one.

### 1. 案例信息

Include material name/ID, platform, date range, screenshots/video attachments, and any user-provided context.

If the user says not to watch or interpret the video, do not infer from the video. Use only the screenshots/data and visible text.

### 2. 爆款逻辑分析

Analyze the creative mechanism:

- Opening sentence type: advice, warning, question, problem statement, tutorial promise, same-style desire.
- Content shell: tutorial, evaluation, sharing, vlog, list, before/after, scenario, short drama, book-list variant, IP/personified account.
- Visual proof: what users see, not what the script claims.
- Audience filtered by the hook.
- Why it could scale: low CPM, high CTR, high CVR, retention, repeatability, or clear audience intent.

### 3. 基础投放数据

If screenshots provide data, calculate only simple derived values that are transparent:

- Clicks = impressions x CTR.
- Conversions = clicks x CVR.
- CPA/CPI = spend / conversions, when conversion definition is known or clearly estimated.
- Relative loss from 3s to 5s = 1 - 5s rate / 3s rate.

Phrase carefully:

- "按截图数据估算..."
- "仍需结合真实转化事件定义判断..."
- "该指标说明...，但不能单独证明..."

### 4. 播放曲线分析

For every curve, look for:

- 0-2s first peak: hook and first visual.
- 3-5s retention: whether the promise is being fulfilled.
- Secondary peak: usually a key visual result, key operation, or surprising product moment.
- Late decline: decide whether to shorten, move proof forward, or use ending only for conversion.

### 5. 可复用点与不可盲目复制点

Always separate:

- What can be mass-produced as volume material.
- What depends on this exact product, user group, or data context.
- What needs a new shell because the current type may saturate.

## 第三节：素材类型拓展与内容策略

### 1. 当前素材类型判断

Do not say "materials are bad" when data shows they work. Distinguish:

- Existing material quality.
- Existing material type diversity.
- Existing audience pool saturation.

Pattern:

`The current issue is not that the materials cannot run, but that runnable materials are concentrated in [current shell]. This shell can be copied for volume, while [ratio] should be reserved for new content-pool/material-type tests.`

If the user gives a ratio such as 20% or 30%, preserve it.

If account bans or delivery restrictions are mentioned, include a rule-library note:

- Risky free/paid language.
- Exaggerated results.
- Sensitive or copyrighted excerpts.
- Platform-prohibited claims.
- Expressions that may reduce account weight.

### 2. 用户本质需求 -> 素材导向

Map product demand into material expression.

For knowledge/content products, do not reduce demand to emotion/social only. Use:

- Self-motivation: self-improvement, growth anxiety, internal friction, discipline, wanting to become better.
- Scene demand: social expression, workplace decision, education anxiety, finance learning, parenting, and concrete knowledge catch-up.

| 需求类型 | 对应用户心理 | 素材表达方向 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-improvement | "I want to improve but lack time/path." | Short-path learning hook |
| Emotion/internal friction | "I need a method, not only comfort." | Emotional hook -> book/product method |
| Scene problem | "I need help in a real situation." | Scenario -> product path -> result |
| Directional learning | "I know what I want to learn." | One problem -> one book/content -> follow-up question |

### 3. 新类型素材测试方向

Write new material directions as testable content shells.

| 新类型 | 内容形式 | 测试目的 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI conversation/demo | Show the user asking a real question and getting a grounded response | Test whether AI can be a core click driver |
| One problem + one content item | One clear problem maps to one book/tool/template/etc. | Strengthen directional use and repeatability |
| High-pain audience series | Separate series for high-value audience groups | Test conversion quality by group |
| Scenario short drama + product solution | Real conflict -> product path -> usable answer/result | Bind product value to life scenes |
| Anti-low-efficiency intake | Compare scattered browsing/collecting with the product's shorter path | Highlight saved time and reduced noise |
| Comment-driven diagnosis | Use comments as future material topics | Improve interaction and source UGC hooks |

### 4. Cross-Category Borrowing

Borrow user psychology and content structures from adjacent categories, not their product details.

When the product has little direct material reference, add a small "同人群同方向需求素材参考" section. In Feishu/Lark docs, use a real `<whiteboard type="mermaid">` mindmap block for the mind map, not an ASCII tree or plain paragraph. Pair it with a migration table for execution details. After insertion, fetch the section and verify that a `<whiteboard token="...">` block exists and that ASCII tree characters (`├`, `└`, `│`) are gone. The rule is:

- First identify the product's core demand, not its category name.
- Find mature products that sell to the same audience or same psychological demand.
- The reference product can be offline/hardware/space-based while this product is online/software-based.
- Borrow the hook, scene, visual proof, and emotional logic; do not copy irrelevant product claims.
- Mark this section as a material-testing reference, not verified performance data.

Recommended table:

| 参考素材品类 | 重合用户/需求 | 可借鉴的素材逻辑 | 迁移到本产品的方式 | 拍摄/投放注意 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware/offline/adjacent product category | Same audience or same demand direction | Hook, scene, visual proof, emotional trigger | How to translate the logic into the app/software product | Risk, compliance, and what not to copy |

For identity/aesthetic/status products, look at apparel, shoes, bags, jewelry, beauty, home decor, room makeover.

For knowledge/self-improvement products, look at:

- Book-list accounts.
- Knowledge-payment/course ads.
- Study/work efficiency accounts.
- Career growth accounts.
- Parenting/education accounts.
- Finance literacy accounts.
- Emotional psychology accounts.
- Podcast/blog clipping accounts.

Translate their structures into product materials without copying claims or unrelated details.

### 5. Evaluation Standards

For new types, do not judge only immediate spend. Track:

- Front-end attention: CTR, 3s play rate, 5s retention.
- Content quality: comments, saves, shares, completion, repeatable format.
- Conversion: CVR, CPA/CPI.
- Strategic value: whether it reaches a new audience or only repeats old users.

## Feishu/Lark Editing Notes

When editing a live Feishu/Lark doc:

- Fetch outline first.
- Fetch only the target section with IDs.
- Use block-level edits for small changes.
- Preserve images, videos, highlights, user edits, citations, and source blocks.
- Do not overwrite the whole document unless explicitly requested.
- After updating, fetch the edited section to verify structure and content.
- When the user expects marked strategic terms, highlight core keywords and definitions with yellow background and red font.
